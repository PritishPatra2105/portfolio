Work Process

How I Work

A simple, structured process designed to turn complex, messy data into high-impact operational dashboards and clear executive decisions.

01.

Understand

Define the core business question, understand operational challenges, and identify the key decisions the analysis must support.

02.

Prepare

Extract, query, clean, and validate raw data using SQL and Power Query, ensuring data accuracy and robust pipelines before modeling.

03.

Analyze

Model relational schemas, engineer DAX measures, and explore patterns, trends, and correlations with SQL and Python.

04.

Communicate

Build clean, intuitive dashboards with responsive visual hierarchies and explain findings in clear, actionable business terms.
