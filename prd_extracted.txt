Layout and typography
Desktop: centered content with a 1200px maximum width and 48px side padding. Use a split hero with text left and optional portrait right; a two-column project grid and four-column process section.
Mobile below 768px: single column, 20px side padding, stacked actions and cards. Tablet uses 32px padding. Content must fit at 320px without horizontal scrolling.
Hero display text scales from 48px on mobile to 112px on desktop. Section headings scale from 32px to 56px. Body text is 16–18px with 1.5–1.65 line height; avoid condensed fonts for long text.
Use 8px spacing increments, section padding of 64px mobile and 96px desktop, restrained 8–12px card radii and thin borders. Keep orange as a focused accent outside the hero.
Composition and imagery
Hero: orange background, black typography and optional authentic portrait with a dark treatment. About and Projects use dark backgrounds; Skills and Experience use off-white panels. How I Work uses four panels with Prepare highlighted in orange. Replace the screenshot’s motion-work gallery with actual dashboard screenshots.
No portrait or dashboard screenshot was supplied. Use a text-led hero and neutral project placeholders during implementation. Do not create fake charts that imply project results. Remove placeholders or label unavailable evidence clearly before launch.
Interaction states
Buttons have hover, focus and pressed states; use black text on orange buttons. Cards use a subtle border change and no more than 4px movement. A visible focus outline must remain against both light and dark surfaces. Motion is brief and disabled with reduced-motion preferences.
Readability
Verify normal text at 4.5:1 contrast and large text at 3:1. Orange text on off-white is limited to decorative or sufficiently large headings unless it passes contrast; use dark text for small labels. The reference’s tiny labels and tight hero layering must be enlarged and separated for readability.
